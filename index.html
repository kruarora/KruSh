The two of us

For anyone who met one of us for the first time at the wedding.

The groom

Krutarth

AKA the calm-on-the-outside, ideas-always-loading, low-key competitive one

Kru is the kind of person who looks calm on the outside but there’s always something quietly brewing in his head. A plan, an idea, a better way to do something… or at the very least, a strong opinion about it.

He’s naturally curious, a little stubborn in the best way, and the friend people turn to when they need both honest advice and someone to actually do something about a problem. He’s equally at home planning a spontaneous road trip, getting way too competitive over a simple game, or going down a rabbit hole just because something caught his interest.

Kru has a dry sense of humour that may sneak up on you. One minute you’re having a normal conversation, the next he’ll deliver a random “Yo Yo Honey Singh” and move on like nothing happened. He’s steady, thoughtful, and deeply loyal to the people he cares about. And when it comes to Shagun, he’s all in: her biggest fan, her safe place, and the person who will always show up, no matter what.

  • 💻Business Analyst, professional overthinker
  • ♍️Virgo

The bride

Shagun

AKA the effortless glow, heart of gold, everyone’s favourite person in the room

Shagun is the kind of person who makes the world feel lighter just by being in it. Effortlessly warm, endlessly charming, and somehow always glowing (seriously, we’re all trying to figure out how), she brings a spark into every room she walks into.

She balances grace with a playful spirit: someone who can be deeply caring and grounding, while also being the reason behind half the laughter in any group. Whether it’s through her sense of style, her energy, or the way she connects with people, Shagun has a way of making moments feel special without even trying.

To Kru, she’s everything he didn’t know he needed: his calm in the chaos, his chaos in the calm, his biggest cheerleader, and occasionally the only person who can successfully tell him to stop overthinking.

  • 🏙️Punjab → British Columbia
  • 💻Consultant and Case Manager, drama gold medalist
  • ♐️Sagittarius

Somewhere between bad jokes, endless Instagram reels, daily Wordle streaks, logic and lightness, spreadsheets and spontaneity, they found each other. 🥂

Thank you for travelling, dancing, and staying up for the pheras with us.

With love, Krutarth & Shagun
